3-Day Split Program

Push / Pull / Legs (PPL) Training Program

EQUIPMENT: Dumbbells & Bodyweight

EXERCISES
13
SPLIT
3 Days
FOCUS
Hypertrophy
DAY 1 4 Exercises

Push Day

Chest, Shoulders & Triceps

DAY 2 4 Exercises

Pull Day

Back, Rear Delts & Biceps

DAY 3 5 Exercises

Legs & Core Day

Quads, Hamstrings, Calves & Abs

Day 1: Push Day

Done Exercise Sets Reps Target Muscle
Dumbbell Floor Press 4 10 - 12 Chest
Dumbbell Overhead Shoulder Press 3 10 - 12 Shoulders
Dumbbell Lateral Raises 3 12 - 15 Side Delts
Overhead Dumbbell Triceps Extension 3 12 - 15 Triceps

Day 2: Pull Day

Done Exercise Sets Reps Target Muscle
Single-Arm Dumbbell Rows 4 10 - 12 Lats / Back
Dumbbell Reverse Flyes 3 12 - 15 Rear Delts
Dumbbell Bicep Curls 3 10 - 12 Biceps
Dumbbell Hammer Curls 3 10 - 12 Forearms

Day 3: Leg & Core Day

Done Exercise Sets Reps Target Muscle
Dumbbell Goblet Squats 4 10 - 12 Quadriceps
Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts 4 10 - 12 Hamstrings
Dumbbell Walking Lunges 3 10 per leg Quads & Glutes
Standing Calf Raises 4 15 - 20 Calves
Plank Hold 3 60 seconds Core / Abs
